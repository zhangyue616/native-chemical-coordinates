# New chemistry confirmation B: data rule

> English reader translation of [`CHEMISTRY_DATA_PROTOCOL.md`](CHEMISTRY_DATA_PROTOCOL.md), added for this public release. It is not a study record: the Chinese file is the record of this protocol and governs if the two differ.

Status: fixed on 2026-10-17 before the first decompression/sampling of the new parent corpus; the research operator is responsible for writing `CHEM_DATA_RULE_LOCK.json`, and execution takes place only after that. The research operator has authorized a new single-MTR confirmation with D=5,000, T=10,000. The predefined eligibility checks on molecule input may be applied at the build stage; concept labels, activations, outputs, unit selection and summaries of T must still not be generated or read until the independent D_OUTPUT_LOCK.

## Source, sampling, and exclusion of old data

The sole public source and its version identity follow `CHEMISTRY_SOURCE_RULE.md`: PubChem Compound/Extras/CID-SMILES.gz, 1,486,837,335 bytes, SHA256 `36b683a59272b196ed7ece140903a249f7df475a06c312c963a5c761f1037f1a`. The manner in which the source was received this time is recorded by the real receipt; a cache copy must not be written up as a network download. The old-study exclusion comes from the 50,000 CIDs and 50,000 saved canonical SMILES identity hashes in `intake/old50k_exclusion/old_50k_exclusion.tsv`; its receipt has been verified by the research operator, and the old 50k or old-study labels/activations must not be read again.

The new salt is ASCII `native-axis-transfer-20261017-v1:`. The full SHA256(salt+CID) is computed on the decimal text of the original CID. Those for which the integer of the first 12 hex characters modulo 1,000,000 is <2,000 are candidates; the entire locked source is read and sorted ascending by **full SHA256 digest bytes, numeric CID**. The threshold and the order must not be adjusted by concept coverage or any model performance. If the fixed candidates are insufficient, the failure must be reported; the salt/threshold must not be changed and no source may be added.

In the above order, check one by one until there are 15,000 qualifying, non-duplicate molecules; filtered-out counts are reported only for this actually processed prefix. First exclude old CIDs; then standardize, exclude old canonical identities, exclude canonical duplicates of this run, and check the mapper. Among those that pass, positions 0–4,999 are D; positions 5,000–14,999 are T. D/T and old structures are all deduplicated by the SHA256 of the standardized canonical SMILES in UTF-8 without newline. Molecules that fail the mapper do not count toward the qualifying number; this rule is fixed before all new results, and the corresponding population limitation is reported. The whole-library candidate count and the processed-prefix count are kept separate, and the prefix filter-out rate must not be extrapolated to the whole library.

This is a molecule split by CID hash, not a scaffold split, and it cannot prove that the molecules were unseen in MTR pretraining. When two CIDs standardize to the same molecule, only the one earliest in the order is kept.

## Standardization

Lock RDKit 2026.03.6, following the standardization rules of the received and reproducible original v1 `build_corpus.py`: default parse/sanitize; per-fragment sanitize; keep the fragment with the most heavy atoms, with ties going to the first in the original fragment order; RemoveHs; 5–50 heavy atoms and no explicit H retained. Elements are limited to B/C/N/O/F/Si/P/S/Cl/Br/I; exclude radical, isotope, non-tetrahedral extended chiral tag, non-single/double/triple/aromatic bond or unsupported bond direction; exclude the originally frozen tripeptide and nucleoside SMARTS; default MolToSmiles generates the canonical isomeric SMILES, and reparse checks the atom count and the permitted chirality/bonds. Neutralization, tautomer standardization or additional drug-likeness filtering must not be performed. The exact filter definitions and their source are saved, and the old v3 identity must not be rebuilt.

## Atom-position observation gate

The tokenizer of the official fixed revision `66b895cab8adebea0cb59a8effa66b2020f204ca` is used, and the vocabulary, merges or offsets must not be modified. The string input to the model must be exactly the saved canonical string. `SemanticMapper.audit_molecule(..., require_canonical=True, max_tokens=512)` is called, and only after the whole molecule passes does each RDKit atom have one usable element anchor token; atoms must not be dropped locally, truncation must not occur, and equal letter count/order must not be substituted for the semantic mapping.

`unique_element_anchor` is adopted: the source characters at the token's original offset, the complete atom span, the lexical graph and the RDKit atom/adjacency must be consistent; missing/duplicate/reversed-order/cross-atom/UNK/source-text mismatch/graph mismatch/unsupported syntax/over 512 total tokens all exclude the whole molecule. Loss of identity information (for example a C token at a Cl source position) does not by itself cause exclusion, but the source/token spelling, the lost attributes and input collisions are recorded. An identified misalignment of a non-alphabetic syntax token may pass only when it affects no element anchor and there is no other error; failures of the original offset and of the complete offset are kept as they are, and they must not be patched and displacements must not be searched for.

The synthetic engineering evidence `results/revision_20261017/mapper/ENGINEERING_CHECKS.json` 47/47 is reused and is not rerun at every build. This check validates only the implementation boundary; each real molecule still undergoes the above gate once. Positional accuracy does not prove that the encoder preserved chemical identity, complete syntax, original pretraining semantics or the correctness of old v3.

## Concepts and data contact

From the saved `concepts_v1.json`, the fixed 91 concepts are taken by `partition=readable` and source∈{topology,fg_hierarchy,fragment_descriptors}, keeping the original ID/name/SMARTS or function definition; the 82 Ertl empirical types, 26 input attributes and 1 input-equivalent concept are not included. Only the definitions are copied, and old prevalence/n_pos must not be treated as new data. SMARTS are matched on the exact canonical graph, `uniquify=True,maxMatches=100000`, and the label is the union of all match atoms; a function follows the frozen topology definition, including the original naming restriction "at least two RDKit rings".

D labels may be generated; the gate by which D determines C is ≥100 positive molecules, ≥100 all-negative molecules and ≥200 positive atoms per concept. All qualifying concepts enter C, in original ID order, and must not be picked by network effect; if fewer than 12, the data coverage gate fails. The input panel of at most 12 is obtained by round-robin class by class over topology→fg_hierarchy→fragment_descriptors with IDs ascending within each class, and must not be filtered by j effect. The mechanism candidates are those of the fragment_descriptors in C that are the first 3 by ID and have ≥200 each of D positive/negative molecules; for those that subsequently fail the association or dose gate, later concepts must not be picked as replacements.

Build outputs include D.tsv, sealed/T.tsv, per-molecule anchor/identity-loss counts, source/standardization/mapper/old-exclusion/input-table identities and the actual filtered-out counts. The T file saves only the exact molecule identities and the predefined input-gate evidence; concept labels or encoder/head functions must not be called. D generates labels, atoms, candidate concept coverage and the panel, and saves each SMARTS's per-molecule unique match occurrence count for the later D association of anonymous outputs; this count differs from the size of the union of atom labels. A topology function saves only a positive-atom count, with its type made explicit. The build may record the actual token input_ids fingerprint and the within-run duplicate/cross-D-T collision counts, but this must not change the locked canonical inclusion/exclusion, and token collisions must not be disguised as chemical identity duplicates or new findings. T labels/concept counts/activations/outputs are generated once, and only after the later D_OUTPUT_LOCK binds the concepts, winners, readouts, mechanism objects, precision and CPU budget.

## Execution and faults

First prepare the source intake, the frozen implementation and the finite synthetic sample/exclusion/split/schema checks, then write the local DATA_RULE_LOCK. Drift in the source SHA, version or implementation stops the run; a missing source or mapper gate must not be silently downgraded. One build may keep intermediate candidates and progress to cope with a purely engineering failure, but must not redraw the salt or change the filtering on the basis of scientific results already seen. The call counts of all D/T raw scientific statistics, forwards and fits are recorded separately by each later stage; for the pool-construction stage they are 0.
