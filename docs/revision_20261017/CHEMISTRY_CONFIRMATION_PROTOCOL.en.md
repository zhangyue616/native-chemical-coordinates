## Object of study and claim

The sole trained checkpoint is DeepChem/ChemBERTa-77M-MTR revision `66b895cab8adebea0cb59a8effa66b2020f204ca`, weights SHA256 `7882205587d3985c07e425632d0e57ba0fc2fc778e3b47aab02aef9b7961407f`. The reason for the selection is the auditable small three-layer encoder and the recoverable anonymous regression head, not the smallest p in old results. The second-to-last layer (0-based1) has subsequent attention to CLS, and the old v4 results of this layer have already been seen; this is explicitly treated as a development prior.

Observation, selection and intervention are performed on the actual FFN post-GELU vector a (464 dimensions) of this layer. The native candidates are all (+a_j,-a_j), with the positive pole before the negative pole and j ascending from 0, 928 in total; each rotation candidate is ±(aQ)_j, likewise 928. Q is obtained by generating a 464×464 standard Gaussian matrix with NumPy PCG64 default_rng at seed30000–30008, applying float64 QR, and unifying the columns of Q by the signs of the diagonal of R; the matrices and the generating code are saved; scoring uses the locked dtype, and all networks share the same 9 matrices. It is an invertible change of basis of the same actual activation space; it has the same linear map only when the output weights are compensated at the same time, and it must not be claimed that an uncompensated rotation preserves the full model function.

The Torch seeds of the three configuration/architecture-matched initialization references are fixed at 21000, 21001, 21002. They are reported one by one, and the three initializations must not be treated as training-randomization replicates. The initialization construction algorithm and the Torch/Transformers versions are locked with the implementation; old activations must not be loaded and old caches must not be used to supplement these new molecules. The sole trained checkpoint, the three seeds and the nine Qs are all fixed; the intervals are conditional on them and on the selected D, and do not include checkpoint-population/training-causal uncertainty.

Historical v3/v4 used the asymmetric (GELU(z),-z) on the preactivation; the ±a of the new lane differs from it. The old eight-model atlas, the 39 references and the posthoc keep their original identity; the new lane must not be used to retroactively ratify missing historical sources or to reverse-repair the historical FWER. The known released-tokenizer identity loss is a reproduction limitation and must not be presented as a new contribution; in this project, the atom-by-atom position audit and identity preservation are kept separate.

## Fixed winners, primary estimate, and statistical family

All qualifying concepts C generated by the data rule on D=5,000 are the primary set. For each network m, each basis b and each c, the unit/pole is selected on D by maximizing standard tie-aware average precision (AP). Equal AP is broken by ascending unit, positive pole first; all networks/bases have the same candidate space, the same D and the same labels. AP weights the precision at each distinct score threshold by the increment in positive-example recall; trapezoidal PR-AUC must not be used. The AP of a constant score equals the prevalence; when a label has no positives, AP is NA, and no epsilon may be added. The implementation identifies actual numerical ties only through deterministic minimal floating-point quantities, and must not artificially merge close scores into one group.

On T=10,000 only the D-fixed winners are evaluated; let

`ell[m,b,c] = log(AP_T[m,b,c] / prevalence_T[c])`;
`B[m,c] = ell[m,native,c] - median_r ell[m,Q_r,c]`;
`Theta = mean_c (B[trained,c] - median_k B[init_k,c])`;
`G_init = mean_c (ell[trained,native,c] - median_k ell[init_k,native,c])`.

Here the median over the fixed references is taken first for each concept, and then the concepts are averaged with equal weight; this must not be mixed up with "the model-population mean of the medians". Negative values must not be truncated. Theta is the primary estimate and G_init is a necessary condition of the joint claim; only when the lower bound of the two-sided 95% interval of each of the two is >0 may the result be qualified as "this trained checkpoint, in the T transfer of D-fixed coordinates, has a native advantage beyond these three initialization references". The two are intersection–union necessary conditions; picking whichever one is positive is not permitted. It must not be called a general training causal effect or a regularity of all models, and it must not be said that all units have semantics.

Each frozen concept on T must have ≥20 positive molecules, ≥100 all-negative molecules and ≥50 positive atoms. A shortfall in any one item leaves the joint primary confirmation event gate unmet; failed concepts must not be deleted in order to recompute a passing set, and T must not be extended. The complete per-concept report includes event counts and the largest single-molecule positive-atom contribution. A concept without positive AP is NA, and statistics that have not passed the gate are descriptive only.

Intervals use 2,000 molecule-cluster bootstrap replicates, seed42017; each replicate draws 10,000 molecules with replacement, carrying all atoms, all overlapping labels and all fixed scalars, and the same resampling is used for all comparisons. Weighted tie-aware AP recomputes the prevalence, the medians and the summaries; D winners must not be reselected, and concepts must not be treated as independent replicates. If any concept having no positive leaves a replicate undefined, the proportion must be reported; if >1%, the interval is unreliable and the joint claim does not pass; if ≤1%, the number removed and the conditional interval must be explicitly reported. Fixed-scalar sort orders/tie groups may be cached; atom-IID bootstrap or resampling of concept means alone must not be substituted.

Oracle separately reselects for each network/basis/concept on the same T and the same full 928 candidates; only point estimates and oracle-minus-fixed differences are reported. All networks/bases are treated symmetrically. oracle≥fixed is a fact of construction, and its difference must not by itself be treated as an innovation; the difference between oracle Theta and fixed Theta is not necessarily positive. oracle must not launch another set of confirmation or reselection claims.

## Finite local input reference

The panel has at most 12 concepts under the data rule and must not be selected by network effect. All readouts use only D; smoothing, categories or binning must not be tuned on T. There is no MLP/target-SMARTS input/additional search. Each concept is given the AP and coverage limitations of the token, radius0 and radius1 references; the principal secondary quantity of conditional information is the **same-T paired Brier increment** between radius1 and radius1+fixed native j.

The radius0 category is (element atomic number, formal charge, aromatic). The radius1 category is the center (element,charge,aromatic,total H,hybridization) and the sorted multiset of neighbors; each neighbor comprises the bond type and the neighbor's (element,charge,aromatic,total H,hybridization). Target matches/SMARTS outputs, target functional-group names, whole-molecule descriptors or learned label-generating features must not be used. radius1 is a finite local nonlinear categorical reference and can directly express some concepts, which is precisely the input explanation under test; it does not represent all input information. It takes its values from the original SMILES graph and may retain more identity information than the lossy tokenizer; this must be disclosed.

The smoothing strength is fixed at alpha=20. Token-category and radius0 frequencies are both smoothed toward the D global atom prevalence. The baseline of radius1 category g is `p_L(g)=(s_g+20*p_0(g))/(n_g+20)`, where p0 is the radius0 reference of its center; a radius1 unseen in D falls back to radius0, and a radius0 unseen in D falls back to global pi.

For each concept, the joint readout uses the all-D-atom quintile thresholds of its D-fixed trained/native scalar (0.2/0.4/0.6/0.8, with duplicate thresholds merged; fixed searchsorted side). Within radius1 category g, for an observed j-bin b, set `alpha_gb=20*n_gb/n_g`, `p_Lj(g,b)=(s_gb+alpha_gb*p_0(g))/(n_gb+alpha_gb)`; an unseen combination falls back to p_L, and an unseen g likewise falls back to the same p0. This choice makes the joint readout exactly equal to the baseline when j is constant, avoiding a spurious increment arising merely from repeated smoothing changing the regularization. Per-category/per-combination counts, the actual bins, unseen categories and fallback proportions must be reported. Binning thresholds/categories/frequency tables must not change after the D lock.

`Delta_L_panel = mean_c mean_atoms_T [(y_c-p_L)^2-(y_c-p_Lj)^2]`. Atom pool weighting is consistent with the primary AP; the molecule cluster bootstrap still carries all atoms, and a two-sided 95% secondary interval is reported. It is independent of the Theta/G_init joint family and must not be used to rescue an unsupported primary claim. Significance must not be declared for the 12 concepts one by one; per-concept results are descriptive and are fully reported. Token/radius0 and AP comparisons are also descriptive. Beating this finite L does not amount to creating information beyond the input; when L has no increment or the CI is wide, this must be retained as it is.

## D precision check

In the fixed hash order of D, the first 4,000 are D_fit and the last 1,000 are D_precision. C is still determined by the predefined coverage rule on all of D; only D_fit is used to select temporary winners for all networks/bases, and 500 molecule bootstrap replicates (seed42016) are run on D_precision, without touching T. The maximum two-sided half-width of Theta and G_init, multiplied by 1.25 and sqrt(1000/10000), estimates the operational expected half-width of the 10k T. The predefined target is log(1.10)≈0.09531 (about 10% multiplicative contrast), which is only a design precision and not a biological meaning.

If the undefined proportion of the D precision check is >1% or the estimated half-width exceeds the target, report the actual values and stop T unsealing, and hand over to the research operator to judge whether this fixed budget is insufficient; C/layer/seed must not be changed, and T must not be increased until significance is reached. Even a pass does not guarantee the real T interval width. After a pass, the sole final winners are selected anew on all of D5k; D_precision belongs to development data and must no longer be called independent confirmation. The finite input and the effect objects are fixed on all of D and written in full into D_OUTPUT_LOCK.

## Anonymous head and minimal effect

Following the fixed official source, restore position0→dropout→Linear384→ReLU→dropout→Linear199; eval turns dropout off. Checkpoint norm buffers are used; by default the normalized anonymous index q is recorded, with denormalized values listed separately. The official 199 descriptor names/order have not been recovered, and an empirical rho must not be named as an official task.

The mechanism candidates are at most 3 frag concepts preselected by the data rule; the trained/native fixed winners of the same primary layer are used. The q with the largest |rho| is selected by the Spearman correlation, on D molecules, between this SMARTS's unique occurrence count and the 199 anonymous normalized outputs (ties go to the lowest q), and the sign is locked; when |rho|<0.5 or the count/output is constant, the mechanism association gate of this candidate fails, and later concepts must not be selected as replacements. The 199 selections and repeated q/repeated unit must be reported, and repeated objects must not be treated as independent mechanisms.

The intervention is at the real post-GELU a point: only the specified coordinate at all atom anchor positions is changed, `a'_j=(1-alpha)*a_j+alpha*mu_D_atom,j`, and positions must not be selected by true-positive atom labels. The primary alpha=1; selected-only alpha=.5 is descriptive. CLS-only independently uses mu_D_CLS and must not be merged with atom-only. Neither special nor PAD may enter the atom mask.

Each selected unit is matched on D-atom mean|a|, SD(a), and the output dense W column norm; the ratios of the first 3 quantities are in [0.5,2], the real residual dose=SD(a)*||W[:,j]|| ratio is in [0.8,1.25], numerical near-zero uses a fixed 1e-12 tolerance, and all selected units are excluded. From the qualifying pool, 5 controls are drawn without replacement with seed44000+original conceptID; if fewer than 5, the mechanism matching gate of this candidate fails, and the caliper must not be relaxed. The intervention uses each control's own D mean. The actual T dose and the degree of D matching are fully reported, and there must be no rematching on the basis of T.

On T, in CID SHA order under the new fixed mechanism salt `native-axis-mechanism-20261017-v1:`, take for each candidate at most 200 concept-positive molecules and 200 all-negative molecules, at least 50 of each; if fewer, the mechanism event gate fails, and the pool must not be topped up. Baseline outputs reuse the complete T pass. Per concept ≤400×(selected+5 controls full-dose atom-only + selected half-dose atom-only + selected full-dose CLS-only)=3,200 molecule evaluations, at most 3 concepts 9,600; in addition, at most 400 final-layer atom-only structural-zero engineering controls, with a total additional ceiling of 10,000. The prerequisite synthetic sham and the last-layer structural zero must not be treated as chemical findings. The primary-layer atom→next-layer attention→CLS path must be actually verified.

The per-molecule change in q output is delta=y_intervened-y_baseline. mean|delta| and signed mean are reported, and |mean delta| must not be used interchangeably with them. The predefined effect quantity is `H_c=(mean_pos|delta_selected|-mean_neg|delta_selected|)-median_control(mean_pos|delta_control|-mean_neg|delta_control|)`. Molecules are bootstrapped separately within the positive and negative strata, seed45017, 2,000 replicates; with at most 3 candidates, H uses a fixed Bonferroni two-sided 98.3333% interval (not reduced to 95% even if fewer than 3 succeed); the same-direction T Spearman association of q is also reported with an interval on the same basis, and an official descriptor must never be named. Without a positive lower bound, only the effect and the uncertainty may be described. Even if supported, it is a computational sensitivity of the frozen model and a chemical conditional association, not a chemical counterfactual of removing a functional group or a general biochemical mechanism.

## Implementation budget and integrity

The base encoder volume is 4×15,000=60,000 molecule evaluations; rotation reuses the same activations, but AP sorting and the bootstrap have a separate CPU cost, and they must not be counted as free. MTR has completed a synthetic small CPU speed test and the head/mask/batch/PAD/sham/position engineering gates; the 84token group measured about 190 molecules/second, and targeted timing is done separately for the permitted 512 total-token window, without repeating the whole set of checks. The research operator has approved a cumulative total of no more than 4 CPU hours for model extraction and intervention and a cumulative total of no more than 12 CPU hours for AP/rotation/cluster bootstrap statistics; process CPU time is used for accumulation, and wall time is reported at the same time. Each process is limited to 4 compute threads to avoid excessive parallel occupancy, and download/pool construction is recorded separately. The above is a hard stop policy with margin and must not be taken as an expected runtime; CPU must not be estimated from old GPU logs, and performance optimization must not change the statistics or undercount replicates.

Numerical contract: the a cache/native score is float32; Q is saved as float64 and converted to float32 at scoring time for a@Q; AP accumulates in float64. Score ties are exact equality of actual float32 values; the absolute tie tolerance for winner AP is 8×float64 epsilon, still with lower unit/positive first. Bootstrap quantiles are fixed to NumPy quantile method=linear; the half-width of each of the two D items is (q.975−q.025)/2, after which the maximum is taken.

D and T have independent caches, recording the identity binding of the input table, checkpoint, implementation, version, seed, layer, dtype and atom anchors. Resuming the same cache after a purely engineering interruption is permitted, without changing the model or the statistical contract. After the first unsealing of T, the primary C/winner/panel/output/control/threshold/sample size must not be adjusted and unfavorable molecules must not be deleted; an actual error requires retaining the failure evidence, a targeted fix, and a statement of the scope of impact. root is the sole party responsible for acceptance in the final integration major stage and reuses this round's engineering evidence; a local engineering PASS must not be upgraded to scientific PASS/author approval/submission.
