## Prediction without fitting and fixed four-term inference

Strictly reuse `krr.pair_data`, retaining the original X64 definition, length L/256, the local 30 positions with 21 states, and PAD; the a/b order comes from the sealed canonical q ascending order. The input j is the z1246 difference between the two separately masked centers. Only call `predict_saved` for the six models (X/Xj/S/Sj/L/Lj), freezing D alpha, lambda, and each block's D trace, and never updating T scales or fitting. Saving fullZ as a cache is allowed, but there is no full-Z readout; there is no additional H regression, full-Z oracle, common lambda, or bootstrap.

Reuse `paired_contributions`, `group_average`, `future_primary_summary`: gX/gS/gL one-sided greater, psi two-sided, Holm fixed at four terms alpha=.05; an untestable term has p=1 and remains in the four-term family. Average within groups first, then weight groups equally; report raw per-pair/per-group contributions, four-term mean/SD/SE, raw p/Holm p, 95% estimate CI, and the per-term 98.75% Bonferroni two-sided t reference CI. Additionally report per term the actual simultaneous CI half-width and whether it is ≤.01; the short-D planning must not be used to claim that long T necessarily meets the target. known-graph isolation does not guarantee no remote-homology or pretraining overlap, and t inference is a reference approximation; sides must not be switched according to results and analyses must not be added. Non-support of gS/gL must not be interpreted as equivalent to no information.

Before each forward, check the CPU used and a reserve of twice the largest previous single call; the stage watchdog checks this process's CPU every 50ms, and on exhaustion writes terminal STOP and exits that scientific process, with no automatic restart. The 50ms detection has a corresponding scheduling granularity, and the budget must not be described as a hard real-time deadline.
